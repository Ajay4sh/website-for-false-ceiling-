FAQ

Common questions

Which is better, gypsum or POP?

Gypsum is faster to install, cleaner, and gives a uniform finish. POP is better for intricate curves and custom mouldings. Many designs combine both.

How long does installation take?

A standard 12×10 ft room usually takes 2–4 days including lighting and painting. Larger or more complex designs take longer. We give you a timeline with your quote.

How much ceiling height will I lose?

Typically 4–6 inches for a single-level ceiling, and a bit more where there are cove or multi-level sections. We'll advise based on your room height.

Can a false ceiling be installed in a bathroom or kitchen?

Yes. We recommend PVC or moisture-resistant gypsum boards for wet areas.

Do you handle the electrical and lighting work?

Yes. Lights, cove strips and wiring are planned and installed along with the ceiling, so you deal with one team.

Is the site visit really free?

Yes. Measurement and consultation are free with no obligation to book.
