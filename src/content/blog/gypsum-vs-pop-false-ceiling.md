Gypsum and POP (Plaster of Paris) are the two most common false ceiling materials in Indian homes. Both can look excellent. The right choice depends on your design, timeline and budget.

## Quick comparison

| | Gypsum board | POP |
|---|---|---|
| Finish | Very uniform (factory-made boards) | Depends on the skill of the team |
| Design freedom | Straight lines, levels, coves, simple curves | Any shape, including domes, circles and mouldings |
| Installation | Faster, less mess | Slower, more wet work and drying time |
| Cost | Similar; slightly higher material cost | Similar; slightly higher labour |
| Cracks | Less likely when jointed properly | Can crack if poorly mixed or cured |

## When to choose gypsum

- You want a clean, modern look with straight lines and cove lighting.
- You want the work finished quickly with less dust.
- The room is a bedroom, living room or office.

## When to choose POP

- You want curved, circular or highly decorative designs.
- You like classic cornices and mouldings.

## The best of both

Many of our projects combine the two: **gypsum for the main flat areas, POP for decorative details**. You get a reliable finish where it matters and full design freedom where you want it.
