PVC and WPC are the two most popular wall panel materials today. They look similar in photos, but they behave quite differently on the wall.

## Quick comparison

| | PVC panels | WPC panels / louvers |
|---|---|---|
| Material | Plastic (polyvinyl chloride) | Wood fibre + plastic composite |
| Look | Printed finishes: marble, wood, solid colours | Realistic wood feel, fluted and louver profiles |
| Water resistance | Fully waterproof | Highly water-resistant |
| Strength | Lighter, can dent on impact | Thicker and more rigid |
| Termites | Termite-proof | Termite-proof |
| Cost | Lower | Higher |
| Best for | Bathrooms, kitchens, damp walls, budget makeovers | TV walls, bed back walls, premium feature walls, exteriors |

## Choose PVC if…

- the wall has dampness or is in a bathroom, kitchen or utility area
- you want a quick, budget-friendly makeover
- you like marble or glossy finishes

## Choose WPC if…

- it's a feature wall people will see every day, such as the TV or bed back wall
- you want the warmth of wood without wood's maintenance
- you want fluted louvers with profile lighting
- the panel is going outdoors (use exterior-grade WPC)

## Combine them

Many homes use WPC louvers for the living room feature wall and PVC panels in bathrooms and the balcony. Each material goes where it performs best.
