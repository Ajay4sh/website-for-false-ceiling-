## Glass partitions

Glass partitions divide a space without blocking light. They are the standard choice for modern office cabins and meeting rooms, and are popular for shower areas and shopfronts.

## Types we install

- **Frameless toughened glass** with patch fittings or U-channels
- **Aluminium-framed partitions** in black, silver or wood finish
- **Glass with gypsum or panelled lower sections**
- **Shower enclosures** with hinged or sliding doors

## Privacy options

Frosted film, gradient film, or printed film with your company logo can be applied to any glass partition.
