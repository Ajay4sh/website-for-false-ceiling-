## Walls that look perfect

A good paint job starts with good preparation. We fill cracks, apply wall putty or POP punning for a smooth base, prime, and then paint, so the finish is even and lasts.

## Services

- **Wall putty and primer:** smooth base coats for new or old walls.
- **POP punning:** a thin POP layer for a very smooth, level surface.
- **Interior painting:** emulsion, washable and premium finishes from leading brands.
- **Texture paint:** sand, stucco, metallic, concrete and designer textures for feature walls.
- **Ceiling painting:** included with every false ceiling we install.

## Clean and careful

Floors and furniture are covered, and we clean up at the end of each day.
