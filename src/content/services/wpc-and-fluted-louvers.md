## What are louvers?

Louvers are vertical fluted panels that create a rhythm of light and shadow on a wall. **WPC (wood-plastic composite)** louvers look like wood but resist water, termites and warping, making them practical for homes in Indian conditions.

## Where louvers work best

- TV unit walls with hidden profile lights between the flutes
- Bed back walls and headboards
- Room partitions and screens
- Exterior facades, balconies and boundary walls (exterior-grade WPC)
- Continuing from the wall onto the ceiling for a dramatic effect

## Why WPC?

- No polishing or termite treatment needed
- Does not swell in humidity like MDF
- Many wood tones, plus black, grey and white
- Quick, clean installation with matching trims
