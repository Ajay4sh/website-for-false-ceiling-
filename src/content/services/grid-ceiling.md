## What is a grid ceiling?

A grid ceiling uses a suspended T-grid (exposed or concealed) with lay-in tiles, usually 2×2 ft. Tiles can be mineral fibre (acoustic), gypsum or metal.

## Why choose a grid ceiling?

- **Easy access:** any tile can be lifted out to reach wiring, AC ducts or plumbing above.
- **Acoustic comfort:** mineral fibre tiles absorb sound, which suits offices and meeting rooms.
- **Fast for large areas:** a practical choice for commercial floors.
- **Integrated fittings:** 2×2 LED panels, AC grilles and sprinklers fit straight into the grid.

## Where we install grid ceilings

Offices, IT workspaces, banks, clinics, hospitals, schools, showrooms and retail stores.
