## Wall moulding

Wall moulding adds frames, panels and borders to plain walls, creating the classic "panelled" look seen in luxury homes and hotels. It is a low-cost way to make a wall look premium.

## Materials

- **PU (polyurethane) and PS moulding:** lightweight, moisture-resistant and easy to install.
- **POP cornices and mouldings:** custom profiles, made on site.
- **Wooden beading:** for a natural finish.

## Popular designs

- Rectangular frame panels on bedroom and living room walls
- Ceiling cornices where the false ceiling meets the wall
- Skirting, dado rails and door surrounds
- Moulding combined with two-tone paint or wallpaper
