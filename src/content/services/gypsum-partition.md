## What is a gypsum partition?

A gypsum (drywall) partition is a wall made of gypsum boards fixed on both sides of a galvanised steel frame. Once jointed and painted, it looks like a regular plastered wall, but it is built much faster, with almost no wet work or debris.

## Why choose drywall over brick?

- **Speed:** most partitions are ready in a few days.
- **Light weight:** suitable for upper floors and existing buildings.
- **Clean:** no bricks, cement or curing.
- **Sound and fire control:** rock-wool insulation and fire-rated boards are available.
- **Services inside:** wiring and switch boxes go inside the wall.

## Options

- Single or double board on each side
- Moisture-resistant boards for wet areas
- Doors, windows and glass panels within the partition
- Cement board partitions for heavy-duty or exterior use
