## What is a PVC ceiling?

PVC ceilings use lightweight interlocking plastic panels fixed onto a frame. The panels come pre-finished in many colours, wood textures and glossy or matt finishes, so no painting is needed.

## Why choose PVC?

- **Waterproof:** the best choice for bathrooms, kitchens, balconies and areas with leakage risk.
- **Low maintenance:** wipe clean with a damp cloth.
- **Termite-proof:** unlike wood or board-based ceilings.
- **Quick installation:** most bathrooms and balconies are done in a day.
- **Budget-friendly:** one of the most economical false ceiling options.

## Things to know

PVC gives a panelled look with visible lines, so it is best suited to wet areas, utility spaces and shops rather than formal living rooms. Choose good-quality, thicker panels: they stay straight and last longer.
