## Wooden ceilings

Wooden ceilings add warmth and character. We work with solid wood rafters, veneer and laminate panels, and **WPC (wood-plastic composite)**, which gives a wood look while resisting moisture and termites.

Popular styles include wooden rafters over a gypsum base, slatted louvers, and wooden panels around a central cove.

## Metal ceilings

Metal ceilings give a clean, modern and durable finish. Options include **linear strip ceilings**, **baffle ceilings** and **open-cell ceilings**, which are popular in showrooms, lobbies, cafés and offices.

## Why choose wooden or metal?

- A statement ceiling that stands out.
- Long-lasting, low-maintenance materials.
- Works with concealed lighting and spotlights.
- Can be mixed with gypsum for a balanced budget.
