## Wallpaper, done properly

A wallpaper is only as good as the wall behind it. We check for dampness, fill and level the surface, and apply a primer before installing, so the paper sits flat with invisible joints and lasts for years.

## Types of wallpaper

- **Designer rolls:** florals, geometrics, textures and solid colours.
- **Custom-printed wallpaper:** your photo, artwork or company branding, printed to fit your wall.
- **Vinyl and washable wallpaper:** easy to clean, good for kids' rooms and commercial spaces.
- **3D and textured wallpaper:** depth and texture at a lower cost than panelling.

## Things to know

Wallpaper should not be installed on damp walls. If we find dampness during the site visit, we recommend treating it first or choosing PVC panels instead.
