## What is a POP false ceiling?

POP (Plaster of Paris) ceilings are made by applying plaster onto a mesh or frame and shaping it by hand. Because the material is worked wet, a skilled team can create almost any shape: circles, curves, domes, coffers and detailed mouldings.

## Why choose POP?

- **Design freedom:** ideal when you want a unique, curved or decorative ceiling.
- **Classic, premium look:** cornices, borders and mouldings give rooms a rich finish.
- **Smooth surface:** finished with putty and paint like a normal ceiling.
- **Works with gypsum:** we often use gypsum for flat areas and POP for decorative details, which gives you the best of both.

## Things to know

POP involves more wet work than gypsum, so it takes a little longer and needs proper drying time before painting. Workmanship matters a lot, because poor POP work can crack over time. Our team follows correct mixing, framing and curing practices to avoid this.

## Our process

1. Free site visit and design discussion.
2. Framing and mesh fixing at the agreed height.
3. POP application, shaping and mouldings.
4. Drying, sanding, putty, primer and paint.
5. Light fitting, testing and handover.
