## What is cove lighting?

Cove lighting is an LED strip hidden in a recess (the "cove") along the edge of the false ceiling. The light washes softly over the ceiling, making the room feel taller and calmer.

## Lighting we install

- **Cove LED strips:** warm white, neutral, cool white or RGB.
- **Profile lights:** slim aluminium channels with LEDs set flush into the ceiling for sharp lines of light.
- **Spotlights and downlights:** for focused light on artwork, TV walls and work areas.
- **Panel lights:** for offices and grid ceilings.

## Why plan lighting with the ceiling?

Wiring, drivers and cut-outs need to be planned before boarding. When one team does both, the lights end up exactly where you want them, the wiring stays hidden and nothing is cut twice.
