## Transform any wall

Wall panelling covers a plain wall with decorative panels, giving it texture, depth and a premium finish. It is one of the quickest ways to upgrade a living room, bedroom or office, and it hides uneven plaster and wiring at the same time.

## Panel types we install

- **PVC wall panels:** waterproof and budget-friendly, in marble, wood and solid finishes. Good for kitchens, bathrooms and damp walls.
- **Charcoal / carbon panels:** a modern, rich look in wood and stone textures.
- **MDF and laminate panels:** classic grooved or flat panels in any laminate colour.
- **3D wall panels:** sculpted patterns that play with light, ideal for a feature wall.
- **Veneer and wooden panels:** warm, natural finishes for a luxury feel.

## Popular uses

- **TV unit back walls** with panels, louvers and profile lighting
- **Bed back walls** with upholstered or wooden panels
- **Living room feature walls**
- **Office reception and cabin walls**

## Our process

1. Free site visit: we check the wall's condition, dampness and wiring points.
2. Design: you choose the panel type, pattern and any lighting.
3. Framing or direct fixing, depending on the panel type and wall condition.
4. Panel installation with neat edges, trims and cut-outs for switches.
5. Lighting, clean-up and handover.
