## Why acoustic panels?

Hard walls bounce sound around, causing echo and making speech and audio unclear. Acoustic panels absorb that sound, making rooms quieter and more comfortable.

## Options we install

- **Fabric-wrapped panels:** high absorption in any colour.
- **PET (polyester) panels:** lightweight, eco-friendly and available in shapes and grooves.
- **Wooden slat panels:** slats on an acoustic felt backing, giving a modern wood look.
- **Perforated / grooved panels:** wooden or gypsum panels with acoustic backing.

## Where they make a difference

Home theatres, conference rooms, open offices, call centres, studios, classrooms, restaurants and places of worship. For the best results we often combine wall panels with an acoustic ceiling.
