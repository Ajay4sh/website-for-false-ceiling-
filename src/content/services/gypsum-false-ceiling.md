## What is a gypsum false ceiling?

A gypsum false ceiling is made of factory-made gypsum boards screwed onto a galvanised steel (GI) frame hung from your main slab. The joints are taped and finished with jointing compound, then primed and painted, giving a smooth, seamless surface.

It is the most popular choice for homes today because it is quick to install, gives a uniform finish and works well with cove lighting, spotlights and multi-level designs.

## Why choose gypsum?

- **Uniform finish:** boards are factory-made, so the surface is flat and consistent.
- **Faster and cleaner:** there is very little wet work compared with POP, so your home stays cleaner and the job finishes sooner.
- **Fire resistance:** gypsum naturally slows the spread of fire.
- **Thermal and sound comfort:** the air gap above the ceiling helps reduce heat and noise.
- **Hides wiring and AC ducts:** keeps your ceiling neat and lets us plan lighting anywhere.

## Our installation process

1. **Site visit and measurement:** we check the slab, room height, wiring and AC positions.
2. **Design and lighting plan:** you approve the layout, levels and light positions.
3. **Framing:** GI perimeter channels, ceiling sections and intermediate channels are fixed to the slab at the agreed height.
4. **Boarding:** gypsum boards are screwed to the frame, with cut-outs for lights.
5. **Jointing and finishing:** joints are taped and filled, then putty, primer and paint.
6. **Lights and handover:** lights and cove strips are fitted and tested, then we walk through the finished room with you.

## Where gypsum works best

Gypsum suits living rooms, bedrooms, dining areas, offices and shops. For bathrooms, kitchens and other humid areas we use moisture-resistant (MR) gypsum boards, or recommend PVC.
